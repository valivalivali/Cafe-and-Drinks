# Privacy Policy for Café & Drinks

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Café & Drinks ("the app"), developed by Micu Florin Valentin ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. We have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

## 1. The Short Version

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.** The app works entirely on your device.

## 2. Data the App Stores (On Your Device Only)

- **Favorites** — the drinks you heart are saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it.

## 3. Purchases

- The app offers an auto-renewing yearly subscription ("Unlock Everything") processed entirely by **Apple** through the App Store's StoreKit system.
- We never see, receive, or store your payment details, Apple ID, or transaction records.
- For information on how Apple handles your payment data, see Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or tracking software. We do not collect or process:

- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Usage or behavioral data
- Device identifiers
- Any content you create in the app

## 5. Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages, collects no data from anyone, and complies with children's privacy regulations, including COPPA. Since no data is collected, no data about children is ever transmitted.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is **Apple's App Store infrastructure**, used for processing purchases. Apple acts as the data controller for payment information; we receive nothing.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted in this repository with a new effective date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
