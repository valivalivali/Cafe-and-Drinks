# Café & Drinks ☕

**Recipes to make at home** — the iOS app that helps you make your favorite café drinks in your own kitchen.

## About

Café & Drinks turns every popular café order into a clear, tested formula you can brew yourself: exact ratios, quantities that scale to your cup size, and short step-by-step guides — no barista experience needed.

**What's inside:**

- 40 popular café recipes on five shelves — Classics, Iced & Cold, Matcha & Tea, Sweet & Dessert, and Modern Craft
- Exact ingredient amounts that rescale for a Small, Medium, or Large cup
- A step-by-step "How to Make It" guide for every drink
- Cup anatomy diagrams showing every layer — espresso, milk, foam, and more
- Calories and caffeine per cup
- Search by name or ingredient, plus favorites
- Five classic recipes free from day one

## Support

Found a bug, have a question, or an idea for a new drink? Please [open an issue](../../issues/new) in this repository — every report is read and it's the fastest way to get help.

You can also reach the developer directly through the App Store review page once the app is live.

## Privacy

Your privacy matters. Café & Drinks collects **no personal data** — no accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Favorites are stored only on your device, and purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Read the full policy: [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
